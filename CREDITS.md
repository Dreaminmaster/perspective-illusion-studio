# Origin, credits and collaboration

**Original project:** [dotslashsimran/simcraft – perspective-illusion](https://github.com/dotslashsimran/simcraft/tree/main/skills/video/perspective-illusion)

**Original author:** Simran Sachdeva © 2026; licensed under MIT. The base `scramble_night.html` Three.js implementation, render scripts, and sample cut-out assets originate in that repository. This repository contains the unmodified upstream MIT `LICENSE` notice.

**Collaborative adaptation:** The repository owner and ChatGPT worked through a series of iterative critiques and revisions to produce the Tokyo Shibuya-inspired demo and the adapted skill workflow:
- Prominent 2° → 78°/84° flat-to-3D dolly zoom
- Second 3° → 58° lens reveal at ~30° corner angle
- Stronger recognisability of Shibuya, original fictional ads/signage
- Crisp native-frame renderer without motion optical-flow interpolation
- Less aggressive rain/bloom and clearer artefact packaging
- Specific technical QA notes, including known aliasing/flicker

The demo is **stylised Shibuya-inspired art**, not an accurate reconstruction of the real intersection. It is also **not generated solely by a text-to-video model**.
